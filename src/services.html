Digital services

All five come with your library card. Staff at the help desk will show you how to use any of them.

Online catalogue

Open 24 hours
  1. Search by title, author or subject from home or any library computer.
  2. Press "Reserve" on the book you want.
  3. Collect it from the front desk within 3 days.

E-books

Open 24 hours
  1. Sign in with your card number and the 4-digit PIN from the front desk.
  2. Borrow up to 3 e-books at a time.
  3. Each e-book returns itself after 14 days, so there is never a late fine.

Study-room booking

Tue-Sat 09:00-19:00
  1. Book a room for 2 hours at the front desk or by phone.
  2. Each room seats 4 people and has a whiteboard.
  3. Arrive within 15 minutes, or the room goes to the next person.

Printing and scanning

Tue-Sat 09:00-19:30
  1. Black and white printing costs Rs 10 a page.
  2. Colour printing costs Rs 30 a page.
  3. Scanning to your email or a USB drive is free.

Wi-Fi

During opening hours
  1. Join the network named "ReadersHaven-Guest".
  2. Enter your card number on the sign-in page.
  3. Each session lasts 3 hours. Sign in again to continue.
