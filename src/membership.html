Late returns

  1. Each late book costs Rs 20 a day. One book returned 5 days late costs Rs 100.
  2. The fine for one book stops at Rs 500.
  3. You cannot borrow new books while you owe more than Rs 200.
  4. A book 60 days late counts as lost. You pay its price plus Rs 300.
  5. To avoid a fine, renew once by phone or at the desk before the due date. You get 7 more days.
